Wird Bin Jarrah (ورد بن جراح) is a beautifully typeset reader for the daily wird of protection — a collection of Qur'anic verses and supplications recited for morning and evening protection.

ورد بن جراح - ورد مختصر من كلام الله تعالى و كلام سيد البشر.
فيه أذكار متنوعة و حصن المسلم.

Wird Bin Jarrah — a concise wird from the words of Allah, the Exalted, and the words of the master of mankind (peace be upon him). It contains a variety of adhkar and Hisnul Muslim.

The full wird is presented across 43 pages of carefully drawn Arabic calligraphy, faithful to the printed original.

Features:
• Crisp, beautiful rendering of every verse and supplication
• Bookmark to save your reading position
• Daily reminder notifications
• Audio du'a on completing the wird
• Repetition counter for adhkar recited multiple times
• Works fully offline
• Free, with no ads

Perfect for morning azkar, evening azkar, daily dua, ruqya, and Hisnul Muslim supplications.

The app collects no personal data — all settings are stored only on your device.
